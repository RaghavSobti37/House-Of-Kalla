Knorr-Bremse at a Glance.

Making mobility safe, efficient and climate-friendly on roads and railways – that is the mission driving us each and every day at Knorr-Bremse. It has been that way for more than 120 years.

Today the Knorr-Bremse Group, based in Munich, is the global market and technology leader for braking systems and a leading supplier of other innovative solutions for the rail and commercial vehicle industries. As an innovator in our fields, we advance developments in mobility and transport technologies. We see it as our daily mission to make a significant contribution to greater safety, efficiency and reliability on roads and railways. That goes for passenger and goods transport – all over the world.
